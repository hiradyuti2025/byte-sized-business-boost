How to Use Hometown Hub
Your guide to discovering local businesses
1
Explore the Map
Click the Map tab to see all local businesses on an interactive map. Click any pin to see a quick summary, deals, and a View Details button. Use the category chips to filter by type.
2
Browse the Directory
Click Directory to see all businesses as cards. Use the search bar to find by name, or filter by category in the left sidebar. Sort by rating, reviews, or active deals.
3
Save Your Favorites
Click the heart icon on any business card to save it. View all saved businesses under the Saved tab for quick access later.
4
View Business Details
Click any business card or map pin to open its full detail page — see info, active deals, and community reviews. You can also leave your own star rating and review!
5
Support Local & Minority-Owned
Filter by Black-Owned or Minority-Owned categories in the sidebar to specifically support these businesses in your community.
6
Chat with the AI Assistant
Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner to ask the AI anything — "best pizza near me", "open late tonight", "Black-owned restaurants" — and get instant answers.
